## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for foreign trade sales operators, cross-border commercial reps, and SOHO export entrepreneurs who demand unyielding speed, mathematical precision, and professional credibility. Foreign trade quotation workflows are high-stakes transactions involving multi-currency calculations, freight tables, and commercial terms; the interface must feel like an institutional financial terminal fused with the polished elegance of next-generation developer tooling.

The aesthetic philosophy draws directly from the clean, hyper-structured discipline of modern technical SaaS:
- **Pristine Whitespace:** Generous breathing room across structural grids to prevent cognitive overload during complex pricing computations.
- **Architectural Hierarchy:** Strict typographic weight distribution and low-noise chrome that foreground line items, unit economics, and margin data.
- **Uncompromising Restraint:** Zero decorative gradients, no unnecessary neon cues, and zero aesthetic fluff. Form follows clarity.

## Colors

The palette operates under absolute chromatic discipline. It is strictly light-mode only, anchored by pure white canvas, engineered neutral slates, and a single high-precision blue accent.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#1D4ED8`):** Tech Blue. Reserved exclusively for high-intent interactions, active state selections, focus indicators, and primary action triggers.
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white background providing the highest dynamic range for data visibility.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F8FAFC`):** Subtle off-white layer for cards, nested table panels, and toolbar backdrops.
- **Sub-Surface Tier (`#F1F5F9`):** Subdued contrast for input fills, hovered table rows, and active badge backgrounds.
- **Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0`):** Crisp, razor-thin perimeter boundaries that define workspace regions without visually cluttering the viewport.
- **Slate Text Continuum:**
  - Headings & Monetary Totals: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - Primary Body & Field Labels: `#334155` (Slate 700)
  - Secondary Metadata & Inactive Labels: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
  - Placeholders & Disabled Icons: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)

No tertiary accents, contextual green success tints, or purple-tinged radial gradients are permitted. Functional verification is communicated through clear typography and crisp neutral-accent contrast.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes high-speed scannability and structural hierarchy across multi-lingual export environments.

- **Headline System (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric purity with sculpted junctions. Headings leverage tight letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to project architectural solidity and modern software pedigree.
- **Body & Data System (Inter):** Systemic, neutral, and pixel-precise. Used for line items, technical specifications, calculation breakdown tables, and input forms. For tabular numerals, numbers must render with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain vertical decimal alignment across dynamic quotation rows.
- **CJK Localization:** For Chinese language support across cross-border operations, the font stack cascades gracefully into `"PingFang SC"`, `"Heiti SC"`, and `"Microsoft YaHei"`, matching Inter’s optical baseline and proportion.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column grid system paired with strict 8pt base spacing increments for rhythm and visual order.

### Grid Model
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, max-width constrained to 1440px with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` to `2rem` gutters. Complex multi-tier quotations use an asymmetric 8:4 split (8 columns for document generation, 4 columns for real-time AI parameter controls and cost breakdowns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Sidebars fold into overlay sheets or stacked modular containers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` edge margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Quotation tables convert into stacked key-value calculation cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Density is calibrated to preserve high whitespace around major components while retaining operational compactness inside data tables.
- Stack heights and component gaps use `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled inputs and labels, `space-md` (16px) for item grouping, and `space-xl` (40px) between card panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via subtle ambient shadows and low-contrast borders rather than deep directional drop shadows. The depth model feels crisp, lightweight, and modern.

### Stacking Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` ground layer.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Cards):** `#F8FAFC` or `#FFFFFF` bound by a solid `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`. Elevation is anchored by an ambient diffused shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Controls & Hover States):** Dynamic interactive elements elevate with a subtle vertical translate of `-1px` and shadow expansion: `0px 4px 8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 12px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 3 (Dropdowns & Contextual Popovers):** High-density overlays rely on a clean `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`, and structured shadow: `0px 8px 20px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

No heavy colored shadows or dark backdrops are permitted. All depth retains low-opacity slate diffusion to maintain structural precision.

## Shapes

The interface blends generous container radiuses with fully rounded pill-shaped actions, striking a balance between modern visual polish and structural ergonomics.

- **Primary & Secondary Action Controls:** Strict pill form factor (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create unmistakable, magnetic click targets.
- **Status & Parameter Badges:** Pill form factor (`rounded-full`) for concise, compact data labeling.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Smooth, generous rounding (`16px` to `24px` / `rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) to soften dense analytical displays and define distinct functional sections.
- **Inputs & Micro-controls:** Crisp, ergonomic standard (`8px` / `rounded-md`) to ensure legible alignment with adjacent tabular layouts.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#1D4ED8` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography (`font-weight: 600`). Subtle hover transition elevating `-1px` with a soft blue-tinted halo (`0px 4px 14px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.25)`). Active state scales slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp `1px` border in `#E2E8F0` and `#1D4ED8` text. Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` background with `#1D4ED8` border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), transparent background, `#334155` text. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Base surface uses `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` background with a continuous `1px` perimeter border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Outer corner radius is fixed at `20px` (or `16px` in compact viewports). Internal padding standardizes at `24px` (or `16px` on mobile screens).
- Sub-cards or summary panels nested inside quotation canvases feature an unbordered `#F1F5F9` background and `12px` roundedness.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), micro-typography (`11px` to `12px`, uppercase tracking `0.04em`).
- Visual style: `#EFF6FF` background (subtle blue tint), `#1D4ED8` text, and an ultra-subtle `1px` border in `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`, rounded to `8px`.
- Typography: `14px` in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus State: Replaces default outline with an explicit `1px` border in `#1D4ED8` and a uniform `3px` focus ring in `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`.
- Currency & Dimension Appendices: Fixed-width trailing badges styled in `#F1F5F9` with `#64748B` label text.

### Quotation Data Tables
- Header: `#F8FAFC` background, `11px` bold text in `#64748B` with uppercase tracking (`0.04em`), bound by a bottom border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Rows: Transparent background, `1px` bottom border in `#F1F5F9`. Hover state shifts row to `#F8FAFC`.
- Cells: Tabular numerical formatting for quantity, unit cost, customs tariffs, and margin percentages.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkbox: `18px` square with `4px` corner radius. Unchecked state: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`. Checked state: `#1D4ED8` fill with sharp white check glyph.
- Switch: Pill track (`36px` x `20px`) in `#E2E8F0`, transitioning to `#1D4ED8` when active. Thumb is a pure white circular disc (`16px`) with a soft baseline shadow.
